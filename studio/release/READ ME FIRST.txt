Duck Echo Studio
================

Make sound effects and music from short text recipes, without a terminal.

Starting it
-----------
Windows: Start menu > Duck Echo Studio (or the desktop icon, if you ticked
that box when installing).
Linux: double-click DuckEchoStudio.AppImage. The first time, it offers to
add itself to your app menu.

Where things are
----------------
Your sounds and songs:  Documents\Duck Echo  (Linux: ~/Documents/Duck Echo)
Your settings:          %APPDATA%\Duck Echo Studio  (Linux: ~/.config/duck-echo-studio)
Updating or uninstalling the Studio never touches either of them.

New versions
------------
When a new version is out, the Studio shows it at the top of its window.
Press Update: it downloads the new version, checks its SHA-256 fingerprint
against the list published with the release, and puts it in. Nothing about
you is sent. You can turn the check off in Settings.

For AI assistants
-----------------
The Studio comes with Duck Echo's command-line tool and MCP server, ready
to run:
  Windows: duck-echo.exe and duck-echo-mcp.exe, next to DuckEchoStudio.exe.
           Tick "Let terminals and AI assistants find duck-echo" when
           installing to call them by name.
  Linux:   DuckEchoStudio.AppImage cli ...   (the command-line tool)
           DuckEchoStudio.AppImage mcp ...   (the MCP server)

"Windows protected your PC"
---------------------------
The installer is not signed with a paid certificate. Click "More info",
then "Run anyway".
